# Owner anti-slop preferences

These are the owner's own rules and sit on top of the craft skills.

- No decorative mini-diagrams or made-up product UI as artwork: tiny cards,
  labels, nodes, connectors, fake dashboards.
- Do not turn project context into decorative statistics, KPI tiles or
  number-heavy copy. Lead with the problem, the purpose and the human benefit.
  At most one verified, meaningful metric in a project summary; detailed numbers
  belong in the case study. Never invent a figure.
- Rendered UI is the product experience only. No designer or developer
  annotations, architecture or implementation notes, prototype disclaimers,
  workflow explanations or speculative next-step sections unless the brief asks
  for them. Keep necessary instructions, labels, validation and status short.
